A private inventory of your firearm collection

Record every firearm and accessory with its photos, papers and value, and see what your insurance covers. It all stays on your computer, encrypted with your passphrase.

Download HoploDex See what it does

Encrypted

One file, AES-256 encrypted, that only your passphrase opens.

Offline

No account, no cloud, no network connection.

Backed up

Backed up for you, and locked when you step away.

What it does

Every detail, on one page

Serial number, cartridge, value, where it came from, and photos.

Know what your insurance covers

Add your policies. HoploDex flags anything uninsured, under-insured or about to lapse.

The paperwork goes with it

Store photos (JPEG, PNG) and documents (PDF, TIFF, Word, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, CSV, text) with each record: bills of sale, receipts, manuals and appraisals.

Accessories, and what they're on

Optics, lights, suppressors, etc. Each shows its value and the firearm it's on.

Cartridges built in

Pick from over 200 built-in cartridges and the caliber fills itself in. Custom cartridges and calibers work too.

Fills in from your collection

Make, model, caliber, etc. are suggested from what you've already recorded.

Private, and only yours

Your collection opens only with your passphrase. No account, no cloud, no network connection.

Also included

  • Search and group
  • Multiple databases
  • Import and export CSV and Excel
  • In-app document preview

  • Origin, importer, and maker's marks

  • Restore from any backup

  • Automatic locking
  • Light and dark themes
