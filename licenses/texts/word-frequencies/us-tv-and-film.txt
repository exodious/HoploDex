Source: https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn/blob/master/data-scripts/count_wiktionary.py
The strength hint's list of words from US television and film (zxcvbn's
us_tv_and_film) is the most frequent words of Wiktionary's frequency lists for
television and film subtitles, as Dropbox's zxcvbn script read them. It is a
word-frequency list counted mechanically from text: in the United States such
a list is facts, which copyright doesn't protect (Feist Publications v. Rural
Telephone Service, 499 U.S. 340 (1991)), so the license of the counted text
and of the pages it was published on (CC BY-SA 3.0 and the GFDL) doesn't
reach it. It ships on that ground, by the exception to 010 FR-015 recorded on
2026-11-04.
