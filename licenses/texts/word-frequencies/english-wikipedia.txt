Source: https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn/blob/master/data-scripts/count_wikipedia.py
The strength hint's list of English words (zxcvbn's english_wikipedia) is the
30,000 most frequent words of English Wikipedia's articles, counted by Dropbox's
zxcvbn script from a dump of their text. It is a word-frequency list counted
mechanically from text: in the United States such a list is facts, which
copyright doesn't protect (Feist Publications v. Rural Telephone Service, 499
U.S. 340 (1991)), so the license of the counted text (CC BY-SA 3.0 and the
GFDL) doesn't reach it. It ships on that ground, by the exception to 010
FR-015 recorded on 2026-11-04.
